# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-16_

This privacy policy applies to **myNews** ("the app"), a private, non-commercial open-source project.

## Summary

The app does not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal data. No user account is required to use it.

## Data collection

- The app does **not** collect any personal data or usage data.
- The app does **not** use analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- The app does **not** require registration or a user account.
- Any settings or content the app may store locally remain on your device and are never transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

## Catalogue of suggested sources

When you agree, the app downloads the list of suggested sources from catalog.cf-my-news.workers.dev, a server run by this project on Cloudflare. Like any web server, it receives your IP address with the request; nothing else about you is sent. If you decline, this request is never made.

## Third-party services

The app does connect to third-party services (news feeds) that may process user data. This is outside the control of this project.

## Children

Since the app does not collect any data, it does not collect data from children either.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or the app, please open an issue at:
